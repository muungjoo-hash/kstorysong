The 8 vowels: sound, mouth, strokes

Click the big letter or the syllable to hear it. Press “Draw” to watch the stroke order.

ㅏ
sounds like a
아
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Open your mouth wide; relax your tongue.

Strokes: ① down from the top ② short stroke left to right

ㅓ
sounds like eo
어
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Like the “u” in “up”: mouth open, jaw down, lips NOT rounded.

Strokes: ① short stroke left to right ② down from the top

Note: ㅓ is not “o”. Keep your lips relaxed, not round!

ㅗ
sounds like o
오
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Small, round lips, like “o” in “go” (no “w” at the end).

Strokes: ① short stroke down ② long stroke left to right

ㅜ
sounds like u
우
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Round lips pushed forward, like “oo” in “moon”.

Strokes: ① long stroke left to right ② short stroke down

ㅡ
sounds like eu
으
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Lips spread flat, teeth almost closed: say “oo” without rounding your lips.

Strokes: ① one line left to right

ㅣ
sounds like i
이
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Lips spread as if smiling, like “ee” in “see”.

Strokes: ① one line from top to bottom

ㅔ
sounds like e
에
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Mouth half open, like “e” in “bed”.

Strokes: ① short stroke ② vertical ③ second vertical

ㅐ
sounds like ae
애
written with ㅇ

Mouth: Mouth a little wider than ㅔ. Today, 에 and 애 sound almost the same.

Strokes: ① vertical ② short stroke ③ second vertical

ㅇ, the silent placeholder

Vowels that point sideways (ㅏ ㅓ ㅣ ㅔ ㅐ) go to the right of ㅇ. Vowels that lie flat (ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ) go below.

Sounds that are easy to mix up

Click each letter and hear the difference.

어–오
eo vs o

eo: mouth open, lips NOT round. o: lips round.

으–우
eu vs u

eu: lips spread flat. u: lips round, pushed forward.

오–우
o vs u

o: mouth a bit more open. u: lips pushed further forward.
