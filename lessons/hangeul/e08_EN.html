Lesson 8 · 「받침 2」

Final consonants (2): ㄱ ㅂ ㄷ

Click any Korean letter, syllable, word or sentence to hear it. Press “Draw” to see the stroke order.

Like English: book, cup, hot 받침 ㄱ, ㅂ, ㄷ

k

Like “book”

Final ㄱ: the back of the tongue stops the air. Do not release it.

p

Like “cup”

Final ㅂ: close your lips. Do not release it.

t

Like “hot”

Final ㄷ: tongue tip on the gum ridge. Do not release it.

Many letters, one sound 대표음

k

ㄱ ㅋ ㄲ

박 = bak · 부엌 = bu-eok

p

ㅂ ㅍ

밥 = bap · 앞 = ap

t

ㄷ ㅅ ㅆ ㅈ ㅊ ㅌ ㅎ

옷 = ot · 꽃 = kkot · 낮 = nat

Tip: Written differently, but they sound the same.
