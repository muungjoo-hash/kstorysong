Today's grammar

① 누가 + V? (“who ~?”)

누가 means “who” when it is the subject of the sentence. Put it before the verb: 누가 원단 검사를 해요? = “Who inspects the fabric?” To answer, use a name + 이/가: 딘 씨가 해요 (Đình does it). 누가 is the short form of 누구 + 가.

QuestionAnswerMeaning
누가 해요?딘 씨가 해요.Who does it? / Đình does it.
누가 원단 검사를 해요?딘 씨하고 퐁 씨가 해요.Who inspects the fabric? / Đình and Phong do.
누가 불출했어요?쩌우 씨가 불출했어요.Who issued it? / Châu issued it.

In the dialogue

  • d3 그럼 누가 원단 검사를 해요? — Then who inspects the fabric?
  • d4 딘 씨하고 퐁 씨가 원단 검사를 하세요. — Đình and Phong, inspect the fabric.
  • p1 누가 원단 검사를 해요? — Who inspects the fabric?
Watch out! When the person is the object (“to whom”), use 누구를: 누구를 찾아요? (Who are you looking for?). 누가 is only for the subject (the person who does it). Add 씨 after a name to be polite: 딘 씨.

② 무슨 + N? (“what N? / what kind of N?”)

무슨 asks “what (kind of)” before a noun: 무슨 일이에요? = “What is the matter?” 무슨 색이에요? = “What color is it?” It is used for things you do not know at all, and often for problems or events.

PatternExampleMeaning
무슨 + 일무슨 일이에요?What is the matter?
무슨 + 색무슨 색이에요?What color is it?
무슨 + 박스무슨 박스예요?What box is it?

In the dialogue

  • d6 무슨 일이에요? — What is the matter?
  • p2 무슨 일이에요? — What is the matter?
Careful: 무슨 is not 어떤 or 어느. 무슨 asks “what?”, 어떤 asks “what kind?” and 어느 asks “which one?”. Use 무슨 일이에요? when someone brings you a problem.

Quick tips for English speakers

  • The verb comes last. English: “Who inspects the fabric?” Korean: 누가 원단 검사를 해요? (who · fabric · inspection · does).
  • 씨 after a name. 쩌우 씨, 딘 씨 are polite ways to call coworkers. Do not use 씨 for a boss: use the job title (조장님).
  • -ㄹ까요? = “Shall I ~?”. 제가 계속 준비할까요? = “Shall I keep preparing?” It is a polite offer or question.
  • -지요? = “…, right?”. 불출했지요? checks if the other person agrees.
  • Letters. GPM002 is read 지피엠 공공이.

More from the dialogue: particles and patterns

-ㄹ까요? (shall I ~?)

Verb + -ㄹ까요? offers to do something: 준비할까요? = “Shall I prepare?”

제가 계속 준비할까요?

-ㄴ (past modifier)

준비한 고무 밴드 = “the elastic band that (we) prepared”. -ㄴ turns a past verb into a noun modifier.

어제 준비한 고무 밴드

-아/어서 (because / and then)

달라서, 나눠서, 입고돼서 = because it is different / divided / received. The reason comes first.

두 번에 나눠서 입고돼서

-는지 (whether)

맞는지 모르겠대요 = “he does not know whether it is right”.

오더 번호가 맞는지 모르겠대요.

-대요 (he/she says)

모르겠대요 = “he says he does not know”. Use it to pass on someone’s words.

모르겠대요.

-(으)ㄴ 것 같아요 (it seems)

다른 것 같아요 = “it seems different”. A soft guess.

색이 조금 다른 것 같아요.
