Today's grammar

① -(으)면 (review: “if / when ~”)

-(으)면 gives a condition or time: 담다 → 담으면 = “if / when you put it in”. After a vowel -면, after a consonant -으면. In the dialogue it sets the order of work: 다 담으면 두 조로 나누세요 = “When it is all bagged, split into two teams.” With 안 돼요 it forbids: 쓰면 안 돼요 = “you must not use it.”

Verb-(으)면Meaning

쓰다쓰면if you use

In the dialogue

  • d1 피오 에이를 폴리백에 다 담으면 두 조로 나누세요. — When PO A is all in poly bags, split into two teams.
  • d4 아니요, 쓰면 안 돼요. 사무실에서 피오 씨 폴리백을 받으세요. — No, you must not. Get the PO C poly bags from the office.
  • p5 끝나면 보고하세요. — Report when you are done.
Watch out! Do not confuse -(으)면 (if/when) with -고 (and then). 담으면 나누세요 = after it is done, split; 담고 나누세요 = put in and split (two steps).

② -고 (“and / and then”)

-고 joins two actions in order: 담고 넣으세요 = “put it in the bag, and then in the box.” It does not change with the final consonant. Use it with different teams: 한 조는 담고, 다른 조는 마무리하세요.

Careful: The tense and the ending go on the last verb only: 담고 넣었어요 (put in and boxed).

Quick tips for English speakers

  • The verb comes last. English: “Be sure to stick on the sticker.” Korean: 스티커를 꼭 붙이세요.
  • Online sales. 인터넷 판매 uses a different poly bag and one garment per bag.
  • GPS sticker. GPS is written 지피에스 in Korean.
  • Teams. 조 = team; 팀 = a group of goods.
  • Letters. PO is 피오, GPM001 is 지피엠 공공일.
