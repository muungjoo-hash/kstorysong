Today's grammar

① 왜? and -기 때문이에요 (“why?” and “because”)

왜 means “why”. Ask: 왜 + -아/어요? To give a reason, attach -기 때문이에요 to the verb stem: 없다 → 없기 때문이에요 = “it is because there is none”. For something that already happened, put the past before it: 기록하지 않았기 때문이에요 (because we did not record it). For a noun, use -이라서 or N 때문이에요.

QuestionAnswerMeaning
왜 적어요?기록하지 않았기 때문이에요.Why is it less? / Because we did not record it.
왜 없어요?프라이스 택이 없기 때문이에요.Why is there none? / Because there is no price tag.
왜 확인해요?수량이 맞지 않기 때문이에요.Why do you check? / Because the quantity does not match.

In the dialogue

  • d3 왜 검사 수량이 정품 수량보다 적어요? — Why is the inspected quantity smaller than the good-product quantity?
  • d4 워싱 후에 검사 수량을 기록하지 않았기 때문이에요. — Because we did not record the inspected quantity after washing.
  • d5 그래요? 그리고 GPM003은 왜 박스 수량이 프라이스 택 수량보다 많아요? — Is that so? Also, why is the carton quantity for GPM003 higher than the price-tag quantity?
  • d6 일부는 온라인 판매용이라서 프라이스 택이 없기 때문이에요. — Because some of them are for online sales, so they have no price tags.
Watch out! A reason always comes before 때문이에요 and the sentence ends with 때문이에요. A short answer like “기록하지 않았기 때문이에요” answers a 왜 question completely. Do not use -기 때문이에요 to ask a question; use 왜.

② N보다 많아요 / 적어요 (“more / less than N”)

보다 after a noun means “than” and is used to compare. Put the thing you compare against before 보다: 정품 수량보다 적어요 = “it is less than the good-product quantity”. The adjectives 많아요 (is more) and 적어요 (is less) go at the end.

PatternExampleMeaning
A가 B보다 많아요박스 수량이 택 수량보다 많아요.The carton quantity is more than the tag quantity.
A가 B보다 적어요검사 수량이 정품 수량보다 적어요.The inspected quantity is less than the good-product quantity.

In the dialogue

  • d3 왜 검사 수량이 정품 수량보다 적어요? — Why is the inspected quantity smaller than the good-product quantity?
  • d5 그래요? 그리고 GPM003은 왜 박스 수량이 프라이스 택 수량보다 많아요? — Is that so? Also, why is the carton quantity for GPM003 higher than the price-tag quantity?
Careful: 맞다 (to match) is different from 많다 (to be many). Say 수량이 맞지 않아요 for “does not match”, and 수량이 많아요 for “there is a large quantity”.

Quick tips for English speakers

  • The verb comes last. English: “Why is the quantity less?” Korean: 왜 수량이 적어요? (why · quantity · is less).
  • Short and complete. To answer 왜, give the reason and end with 때문이에요. You do not need to repeat the question.
  • Records matter. 기록하다 (to record) is a key work word: 기록을 꼭 하세요 = “Be sure to keep records.”
  • 정품 = good products. Items that passed inspection. Rejected items are 불량품.
  • Item codes. GPM003 is read 지피엠 공공삼.

More from the dialogue: particles and patterns

이리 와 보세요 (come here)

와 보세요 = “please come (and see)”. -아/어 보세요 softens a request or a call.

이리 와 보세요.

-지 않다 (not)

Verb stem + -지 않다 is a longer way to say 안 + verb: 기록하지 않았어요 = 안 기록했어요.

기록하지 않았기 때문이에요.

-은 (the verb describes a noun)

맺은 직원 = “the employee who signed”. The verb comes before the noun it describes.

계약을 맺은 직원 명단

-(으)로 (by / with)

컴퓨터로 = “on the computer”. 로 marks the tool or means.

컴퓨터로 입력해 주세요.

-이라서 (because it is ~)

Noun + -이라서 = “because it is ~”: 판매용이라서 = because it is for sale.

온라인 판매용이라서

알겠어요 (I see)

Said after hearing an explanation. 알겠어요 (polite, casual) and 알겠습니다 (formal) both mean “I understand”.

알겠어요. 다시 확인해 보세요.
