Today's grammar

① -(으)ㄹ까요? (“shall we ~? / may I ~?”)

-(으)ㄹ까요? asks for someone’s opinion or offers to do something. After a vowel use -ㄹ까요? (쓰다 → 쓸까요? “Shall we use it?”); after a consonant use -을까요? (먹다 → 먹을까요?). With -아/어도: 달아도 될까요? = “May I attach it anyway?”

Verb-(으)ㄹ까요?Meaning
쓰다쓸까요?Shall we use it?
먹다먹을까요?Shall we eat?
달다달아도 될까요?May I attach it?
하다할까요?Shall I do it?

In the dialogue

  • d1 반장님, 화이트 에리가 이색이에요. 달아도 될까요? — Sir, the white collar has a shade difference. May we attach it anyway?
  • d8 어느 것을 쓸까요? — Which one shall we use?
Watch out! -(으)ㄹ까요? is softer than -나요?. The answer is a decision: 쓰세요 (use it) or 쓰지 마세요 (do not use it). When you promise to do something yourself, use -(으)ㄹ게요: 확인할게요.

② -(으)ㅂ시다 (“let us ~”)

-(으)ㅂ시다 suggests doing something together: 작업하다 → 작업합시다 = “Let us work.” After a vowel use -ㅂ시다; after a consonant use -읍시다 (먹읍시다). It is used by a manager to propose a group decision.

Verb-(으)ㅂ시다Meaning
작업하다작업합시다Let us work
확인하다확인합시다Let us check
먹다먹읍시다Let us eat

In the dialogue

  • d6 그러면 중단하고 그레이로 작업합시다. — Then let us stop and work on gray.
  • d10 첫 번째 입고된 실로 작업합시다. — Let us work with the thread that came in first.
Careful: -(으)ㅂ시다 is for a suggestion to equals or juniors. Do not use it to a senior; use -(으)시겠어요? or -(으)ㄹ까요? instead.

Quick tips for English speakers

  • The verb comes last. English: “Let us work with gray.” Korean: 그레이로 작업합시다 (gray · with · let us work).
  • -기 전에 = before ~ing. 달기 전에 검사하세요 = inspect before attaching.
  • 이색 = shade difference. Stop and check before you continue.
  • 첫 번째 = the first time / first one. 두 번째 = second, 세 번째 = third.
  • 앞으로는 = from now on. Use it to set a new rule.

More from the dialogue: particles and patterns

-고 (and then)

로 (with / in)

그레이로, 실로 = “with gray”, “with the thread”. 로 marks a material or option.

-(으)ㄹ게요 (I will)

확인할게요 = “I will check.” A promise by the speaker.

-ㄴ (past modifier)
