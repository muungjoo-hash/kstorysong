Today's grammar

① V-아/어도 돼요? (“May I ~? / Is it okay to ~?”)

To ask permission, add -아/어도 돼요? to a verb stem: 통과시켜도 돼요? = “May I let it pass?” Use -아도 after ㅏ or ㅗ and -어도 after other vowels; 하다 verbs become -해도 (확인해도 돼요?). To say yes, answer 네, 돼요 (“Yes, you may”).

In the dialogue

  • d5 조장님, 이 옷은 색이 조금 달라요. 통과시켜도 돼요? — Leader, this garment’s color is a little different. Can I let it pass?
  • p3 통과시켜도 돼요? — Can I let it pass?
Watch out! 돼요 comes from 되다 (to be okay). Do not confuse 돼요 with 되어요 (a longer form) or with 해요 (to do). In speech, listen for the question tone at the end.

② 안 돼요 (“It is not allowed / it is no good”)

안 돼요 means “no, you may not” or “that is not acceptable”. Use it to refuse or prohibit. Add a reason or an instruction after it: 안 돼요. 이 카트에 따로 놓으세요 = “No. Put it separately on this cart.” The polite refusal begins with 아니요 (no).

Careful: 안 돼요 can sound strong. A manager often follows it with what to do instead: 따로 놓으세요 (put it separately).

Quick tips for English speakers

  • The verb comes last. English: “Can I let it pass?” Korean: 통과시켜도 돼요? (let it pass · is it okay).
  • Color words end in 색 or in the plain form. 흰색 (white), 회색 (gray), 갈색 (brown); and 검정, 빨강, 노랑, 파랑, 초록.
  • Numbers on labels. 피오 번호 = PO number, 칼라 번호 = color number, 스타일 번호 = style number.
  • Off-shade = different color. 이색 / 색이 달라요. Ask before you pass it: 통과시켜도 돼요?
  • Letters. GPM001 is read 지피엠 공공일; PO is written 피오 in Korean.

More from the dialogue: particles and patterns

-(으)면 (if)

부족하면 = “if it is short”. Verb stem + -(으)면 gives a condition.

마지막에 수량이 부족하면 다시 확인하세요.

-아/어야 해요 (must)

같아야 해요 = “must be the same”. Use it for rules and requirements.

모든 각이 같아야 해요.

-(으)ㄹ 때 (when)

검사할 때 = “when you inspect”. Use -(으)ㄹ 때 for “when ~”.

검사할 때는 브이 모양을 잘 보세요.

-는지 (whether)

있는지, 번호인지 = whether ~. 모르겠어요 often follows: “I do not know whether ~”.

어느 것이 피오 번호인지 모르겠어요.

-아/어 있다 (state)

달려 있다, 걸려 있다 = is attached / is hanging. -아/어 있다 describes a state.

부자재 카드는 저기 걸려 있어요.

따로 (separately)

따로 놓으세요 = put it separately. Use it for items that must be kept apart.

이 카트에 따로 놓으세요.
