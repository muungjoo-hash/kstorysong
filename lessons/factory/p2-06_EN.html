Today's grammar

① 이것 · 그것 · 저것 (“this / that / that over there”)

Korean has three words for “this / that” that depend on where the object is. 이것 is near the speaker, 그것 is near the listener, and 저것 is far from both. Add 은 (topic marker after a consonant) to make a sentence: 이것은 뭐예요? = “What is this?”

WordExampleMeaning
이것이것은 뭐예요?this — near the speaker. What is this?
그것그것은 택총이에요.that — near the listener. That is a tag gun.
저것저것은 넘버링기예요.that over there — far from both. That is a numbering machine.

In the dialogue

  • d1 이것은 뭐예요? — What is this?
  • d2 그것은 택총이에요. — That is a tag gun.
  • d3 저것은 뭐예요? — What is that over there?
Watch out! 그것 depends on the listener. When you ask “이것은 뭐예요?” about the thing next to you, the other person answers “그것은 ~이에요” because it is near them. If you both look at something far away, use 저것.

② N이에요 / N예요 (“it is N”)

이에요 / 예요 means “is”. Use 이에요 after a noun ending in a consonant (택총이에요) and 예요 after a vowel (넘버링기예요, 뭐예요). It is the everyday polite style; the formal style is 입니다 (Lesson 1).

NounSentenceMeaning
택총 (consonant)택총이에요It is a tag gun.
넘버링기 (vowel)넘버링기예요It is a numbering machine.
계수기 (vowel)계수기예요It is a counting machine.
뭐 (vowel)뭐예요?What is it?

In the dialogue

  • d2 그것은 택총이에요. — That is a tag gun.
  • d4 저것은 넘버링기예요. — That over there is a numbering machine.
  • d3 저것은 뭐예요? — What is that over there?
  • p2 그것은 계수기예요. — That is a counter.
Careful: Say the item name before 이에요/예요: 택총이에요. The sentence order is “A은/는 B이에요/예요” (A is B). Do not add a separate verb for “is”.

Quick tips for English speakers

  • The verb comes last. English: “That is a tag gun.” Korean: 그것은 택총이에요 (that · tag gun · is).
  • Point and ask. The easiest way to learn names at work: point at something and ask 이것은 뭐예요?
  • 다 떨어졌어요 = “it has run out”. Use it whenever supplies are finished.
  • Throw away: 버리다 means to throw away. 쓰레기통에 버리세요 = “Throw it in the trash can.”
  • Borrowed words. Many supply names come from English: 테이프 커터기, 스티커, 컴퓨터, 커피.

More from the dialogue: particles and patterns

은/는 (topic)

After a consonant 은 (이것은), after a vowel 는 (쓰레기는). It marks what you are talking about.

쓰레기는 쓰레기통에 버리세요.

에서 (from / at)

사무실에서 = “from the office”. 에서 marks the place where something starts or happens.

사무실에서 가져오겠습니다.

가져오다 (bring)

가져오다 = go get something and bring it back. With -겠습니다 it is a promise: “I will bring it.”

네, 사무실에서 가져오겠습니다.

떨어지다 (run out)

다 떨어졌어요 = it has all run out. 다 means “all”.

오염제거제가 다 떨어졌어요.

에 (into / at)

쓰레기통에 = “into the trash can”. 에 marks the place something is put.

쓰레기통에 버리세요.

-(으)세요 (command)

Verb + -(으)세요 = a polite command: 버리세요, 정리하세요. You will learn it fully in Lesson 7.

책상을 정리하세요.
